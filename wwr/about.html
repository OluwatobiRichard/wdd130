Whitewater Rafting

Whitewater rafting evolved from military and exploration vessels into a commercial adventure sport, pioneered in the United States. Following World War II, surplus rubber military rafts became widely available, leading to the first commercial trips along Idaho's Salmon River in the 1940s and early 1950s.
